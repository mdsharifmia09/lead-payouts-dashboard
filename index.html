How these numbers were built

Built from the CPL / Duration buyer payout spreadsheet (Plumbing, HVAC, Electrical, Siding, Water Damage, Mold, Roofing, Appliance, Windows, Bathroom, Kitchen, Pest Control, Locksmith, Tree Services). Where a ZIP appears more than once in the same offer, the highest payout is kept. Painting has a ZIP list with no payouts, so it's left out. Combined payout adds CPL and Duration payouts together — use it to rank ZIPs, not as money from one call.
